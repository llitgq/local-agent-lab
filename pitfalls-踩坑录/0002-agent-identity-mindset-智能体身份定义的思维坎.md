## Symptom

I could write extremely rigorous prompts and workflow specs — but the moment an agent had to act on its own, quality collapsed and tasks kept bouncing back to me. That wall cost me one to two weeks.

## Root cause

Years of conversational AI boxed in my thinking: I was used to "writing one good instruction", not **decomposing an agent's identity definition into parts** — role, responsibilities, boundaries, tools, each its own piece.

## Fix

Switch the mental model: from "writing instructions" to "defining identity + decomposing responsibilities". Read other people's agent definition structures and follow the structure instead of forcing old skills onto a new paradigm.

## Prevention (one line)

Entering a new paradigm, learn its definition structure first — then bring your old strengths.
